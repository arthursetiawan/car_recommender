DriveQuest for dealer groups

Embed an honest car advisor scoped to your dealer group inventory — or optionally to one rooftop. Shoppers chat, compare on a hex chart, and check EV break-even without seeing another group’s lot.

Sample groups

One-line install

Group-wide (all rooftops):

<script src="https://YOUR_HOST/widget.js?group=northstar-motors" async></script>

Single location (this store’s site):

<script src="https://YOUR_HOST/widget.js?group=northstar-motors&location=downtown" async></script>
